## Music direction

Warm, soft-percussive, ~110 BPM, no vocals. Build at 0:30 when the timeline draws. **Drop the music out at 0:52** so the privacy line lands in near-silence — that silence is the most persuasive second in the whole video. Bring it back at 0:57 for the CTA.

Royalty-free sources that clear commercial use: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or YouTube Audio Library (free, but check the attribution flag per track).
